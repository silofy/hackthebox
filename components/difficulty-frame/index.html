Difficulty system

Two lines per frame: a muted rail and a bright accent against its inner edge. Each tier adds rail cuts, a longer accent and a heavier animation. Hover a badge, click to hold it selected.

Easy2 inner lines, top and bottom · lines grow in · glow on hover
Medium2 inner lines, left and right · outer line traces in · lines press out on hover
Hard4 inner lines in openings · outer line cracks open, lines lock once · stronger glow
Insane4 longest inner lines · sweep in, lock twice · lines travel on hover

Machine states

Idle, spawning, spawned and de-spawning, set with the state attribute. Completed combines with any of them. The bottom row runs the full lifecycle on a loop.

Completed

A check medallion takes the place of the bottom point; the rest of the tier pattern stays.
